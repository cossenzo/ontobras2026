#ontobras2026
## ONTOBRAS 2026 paper ##
### An Ontological and Methodological Framework for Evidence Synthesis in Mixed-Methods Systematic Reviews ###
**Fábio Cossenzo, Marcello Peixoto Bax**

**Documents available:**
- `OntoLOASx.owl`: OntoLOASx implemented in Protégé in RDF/XML format.
- `create_ontoloasx.cypher`: Cypher script for creating the OntoLOASx ontology in Neo4j Desktop.
- `slr_dataset.txt`: Systematic literature review sample dataset coded from [1], provided as a tab-delimited TXT file.
- `create_ontoloasx_instances.cypher`: Cypher script for creating the OntoLOASx-based sample knowledge graph in Neo4j Desktop.
- `ontoloasx_competency_questions.cypher`: Cypher script for executing the eight OntoLOASx competency questions in Neo4j Desktop.

[1] C. Reagon, N. Gale, S. Enright, M. Mann, and R. Van Deursen, “A mixed-method systematic review to investigate the effect of group singing on health related quality of life,” Complementary Therapies in Medicine, vol. 27, pp. 1–11, Aug. 2016, doi: 10.1016/j.ctim.2016.03.017.
